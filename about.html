The wedding house

PERAN The Wedding House is an atelier in Memnagar: indo-western on rent, designer kurtas, and a Navratri rail that the house refreshes for garba season. The mark is a deep burgundy field with an ivory wordmark. This site keeps that palette and nothing invented beside it.

The shop you are browsing is a prototype. Names and rupee prices are written for the preview where the Instagram captions did not list them. The photographs are not stock: they are frames saved from the public posts on @peran.in, mostly the latest Navratri kurta carousels. Older posts sat behind a login wall and a rate limit, so bridal gowns and decor from earlier seasons are not in this edit.

Visit the floor at F-8, Balaji Centre, opposite Gurukul Temple, Gurukul Road, Memnagar, Ahmedabad 380052. Call 89809 06104. The house also keeps a YouTube channel and a map pin.

How to read the prices

A preview, not a bill.

Adding a piece to the cart and checking out only confirms the form on this device. The atelier is not notified, and no payment is captured. For a real hold, send a screenshot of the kurta on WhatsApp or call the shop, the way the house already asks on Instagram.
